Player Equaliser

Looking for piCorePlayer players…

The graph shows the equaliser's actual effect, worked out from its filters; dots are the slider settings. Neighbouring bands overlap, so a band's effect is wider and often smaller than its slider.

Move a slider by dragging its handle; tap a band's dB value to set it to 0 dB. Flat sets all bands to 0 dB.

Changes play straight away. They are only kept over a restart when you press Save; Reset goes back to the last saved settings. One step is 0.72 dB.

Boosting above flat can distort loud passages. Lowering the other bands instead keeps the same balance with less risk.
